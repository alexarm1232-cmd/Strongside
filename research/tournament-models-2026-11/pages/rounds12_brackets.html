Rounds 1–2 Brackets 2010–2026

Three brackets filled out before Round 1 of every tournament. The vote of the three models is the one in use; the blend and the upset models on their own are shown for comparison. Each picks all 32 Round 1 games, then the Round of 32 game between its own Round 1 winners. Scored against what happened: 1 point per Round 1 game, 2 per Round of 32 game. The models are scored on each year after training on the other 15.

Click a row to show that picker's brackets. Round of 32 right counts a pick only if that team really won its Round of 32 game, so a team that lost in Round 1 is already wrong.
