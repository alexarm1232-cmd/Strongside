Full Brackets 2010–2026

One bracket per tournament, filled out before Round 1 the normal way: every round picks between the bracket’s own winners. Pod bracket (new): Round 1 takes the upset model’s pick unless both other models disagree and are each at least 60% sure. Round of 32 is picked by pod: each team’s chance to win its four-team pod (win Round 1, then beat whoever comes out of the other game, regular model chances); the likeliest pod winner is the pick and wins its Round 1 game. Pod + pod strength (main): the pod bracket, but the Sweet 16 pick also weighs how strong the other three teams in each team’s Round of 32 pod were (pre-tournament net ratings). Vote of the 3: majority of the upset models, regular model and simulator in Rounds 1–2. Sweet 16 and Elite Eight: the regular model. Final Four and title: 70% simulator (10,000 games, pre-tournament, nobody out) + 30% regular model. Scored 1-2-4-8-16-32, 192 points a year at most. Models are scored on each year after training on the other 15.

Click a year to see its bracket. A pick counts only if that team really won that round, so a team already knocked out is a miss.
